Firebase Domain Authorization Required

Google OAuth Security Policy

Why is Google Sign-In asking for this?

Google Firebase protects your users by only allowing sign-ins from authorized domains. Your Render production domain must be added to your Firebase project whitelist.

How to Fix in 10 Seconds (One-Time Setup):
  1. Open the Firebase Console ➔ Authentication Settings.
  2. Click the "Authorized domains" tab.
  3. Click "Add domain", paste iveri-ai-support.onrender.com, and click Save.
