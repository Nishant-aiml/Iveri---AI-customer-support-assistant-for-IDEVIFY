IVERI RETAIL HARDWARE WARRANTY AND SERVICE TERMS

1. Limited Manufacturer Warranty:
All hardware devices and consumer electronics purchased from Iveri Retail come standard with a 1-year (12-month) limited manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase.

2. What Is Covered:
- Factory hardware defects, component failure, and assembly errors
- Internal power supply, battery health dropping below 80% within the first year
- Touchscreen and display pixel anomalies exceeding 3 dead pixels
- Wireless connectivity (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi) hardware failures

3. What Is NOT Covered (Exclusions):
- Accidental physical damage (e.g. cracked screens, drops, dents)
- Water submersion, liquid spill damage, or corrosion
- Unauthorized modifications, firmware jailbreaking, or third-party repair attempts
- Normal aesthetic wear and tear (scratches on casing)

4. Repair Procedure:
To file a warranty claim, contact support with your proof of purchase (invoice number) and device serial number. We will provide a prepaid shipping box. Most repairs or direct certified replacements are completed within 7 to 10 business days.
