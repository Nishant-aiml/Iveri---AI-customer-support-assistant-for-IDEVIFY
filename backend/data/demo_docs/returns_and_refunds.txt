IVERI RETAIL RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

1. Return Window:
Customers may return any eligible product within 30 days of the original delivery date for a 100% full refund to their original payment method. Items returned between 31 and 60 days from delivery are eligible for store credit or product exchange only. No returns or exchanges are accepted after 60 days.

2. Condition of Returned Items:
To qualify for a refund, products must be returned in their original packaging, unwashed, unworn, and with all original tags, accessories, and warranty cards intact.

3. Non-Returnable Items:
The following items are final sale and cannot be returned or refunded:
- Digital gift cards and software licenses
- Personalized or custom-engraved merchandise
- Clearance and open-box items marked 'Final Sale'
- Personal hygiene and sanitary items once opened

4. Return Shipping Fee:
Returns due to manufacturer defects or incorrect items shipped are 100% free; Iveri Retail provides a pre-paid return shipping label. For change-of-mind returns, a flat return shipping and restocking fee of $5.99 will be deducted from the refund amount.

5. Processing Time:
Refunds are inspected and processed within 3 to 5 business days of receiving the package at our central fulfillment warehouse. Banks typically take an additional 2 to 4 business days to reflect the credit on your statement.
