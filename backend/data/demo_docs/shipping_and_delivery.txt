IVERI RETAIL SHIPPING AND DELIVERY GUIDELINES

1. Standard Shipping (Domestic):
- Delivery Timeframe: 3 to 5 business days nationwide.
- Flat Shipping Rate: $4.99 for orders under $49.
- Free Shipping Threshold: All orders with a subtotal of $49.00 or more automatically qualify for FREE standard domestic shipping.

2. Express 2-Day Delivery:
- Delivery Timeframe: 1 to 2 business days if placed before 2:00 PM EST Monday through Friday.
- Flat Rate: $12.99 per order.
- Service is delivered via FedEx 2Day or UPS 2nd Day Air.

3. Overnight Priority Delivery:
- Delivery Timeframe: Next business day delivery guaranteed.
- Flat Rate: $24.99 per order.
- Saturday delivery is available in select metropolitan postal codes for an additional $10 carrier surcharge.

4. International Shipping:
- We currently ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, and India.
- International standard delivery takes 7 to 14 business days.
- Duties and import taxes are calculated at checkout and collected upfront (DDP) so customers experience no surprise customs delays.

5. Order Tracking:
As soon as an order is packed and dispatched, customers receive an automated SMS and email containing a real-time tracking link with live carrier status updates.
